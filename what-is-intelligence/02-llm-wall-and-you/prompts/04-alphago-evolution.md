A wide editorial cartoon illustration in Notion-style flat-vector aesthetic with soft hand-drawn outlines, warm off-white background, generous whitespace, paper-grain texture.

- Mood: traditional, library-like, knowledge-flowing-down.

- Mood: futuristic, self-contained, closed-loop.
