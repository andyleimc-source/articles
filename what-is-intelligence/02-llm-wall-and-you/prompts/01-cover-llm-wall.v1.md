# Cover Prompt — 大模型撞墙了吗？

A wide editorial illustration in Notion-style flat-vector cartoon aesthetic with soft hand-drawn outlines, generous whitespace, subtle paper-grain texture, warm and friendly tone.

Above the wall, in small lettering: a faint label reading "SCALING" in light graphite gray.
