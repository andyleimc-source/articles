# Cover Prompt — AI 帮你打掉了 80% 的工作

A wide editorial illustration in Notion-style flat-vector cartoon aesthetic with soft hand-drawn outlines, generous whitespace, subtle paper-grain texture, warm and friendly tone.

**Scene composition** (centered on the canvas):

A simplified cartoon character (no facial features, just a clean silhouette of a person, viewed from a 3/4 angle) sits at a wooden desk in the middle of the canvas. On the desk are stacks of work items — papers, a laptop, sticky notes, a calendar.

**The split**: A clear visual divider runs horizontally across the desk. The LEFT 80% of the desk's content is being absorbed/flowing toward a stylized AI robot icon (a simple rounded square with two antennae and a soft glowing eye) on the FAR LEFT of the canvas. This flow is rendered as soft arrows in graphite gray, suggesting items being pulled away. The items being absorbed are clearly "routine" work: a stack of PPT slides, an email envelope, a spreadsheet, a code bracket symbol.

The RIGHT 20% of the desk's content is held tightly by the character. This 20% pile is small but glows with a phosphor-green halo. It contains: a stamp (decision), a handshake icon (negotiation), a notebook (reflection/feedback). A label tag in phosphor green reads "决定你的位置".

**Visual proportion**: The 80% pile feeds into the AI on the left and is depicted using ~80% of horizontal canvas width on that side. The 20% green-glowing pile is small but visually highlighted — drawn larger relative to its share to draw the eye.

**Mood**: not threatening. Slightly hopeful. The character looks relaxed, not panicked — like someone who has just realized that "the boring stuff is gone, now I get to do the part that matters."
