# Prompt: yazi cover — terminal duck

A wide 16:9 magazine-style cover illustration in **Notion flat illustration style** —
soft hand-drawn imperfection, gentle wobbly outlines, flat shapes with very subtle
paper grain texture, warm editorial feel, lots of whitespace, light vignette.

## Composition (left-to-right)

- **Center-left**: a vintage retro computer terminal window, large, frontal view.
  - Window chrome rendered in **ink-black #0E1116** thin lines (NOT pure black),
    with the classic three small dots / window controls in faded **mist-blue #9CB4CC**.
  - Inside the terminal screen: a **cream #FAF7F0** background (matching page bg
    so the screen feels like an open viewport, not a dark panel).
  - A small ASCII-style prompt symbol "❯" in **phosphor green #1AB87C** in the top
    corner of the terminal screen, hand-drawn slightly imperfect.
  - Inside the screen, a hand-drawn cute cartoon yellow **duck** (round body,
    simple shapes, friendly tilted head, big black dot eye, small orange beak),
    sitting calmly on a horizontal "shelf line". The duck is the visual anchor.
  - The duck's outline in **ink-black #0E1116** thin pen strokes; body filled
    with a soft pastel yellow that is muted and warm, NOT neon — keep it editorial.
  - **Phosphor-green #1AB87C** small "checkmark" or sparkle accent floating near
    the duck's head, signaling the file-management theme.

- **Around / floating outside the terminal**: 3-4 small "preview thumbnail" cards
  hovering at slight angles around the terminal, like the terminal is showing
  previews. Each card a simple flat rounded-rectangle:
  - One image-thumbnail card: a tiny mountain + sun pictogram (cream bg, ink-black
    outline, phosphor-green accent for the sun).
  - One video-thumbnail card: a simple play-triangle in phosphor green, with a
    subtle film-strip detail at top in graphite #3A4151.
  - One PDF/document card: lines of text suggested by horizontal graphite strokes
    on cream, with a small folded-corner detail.
  - Cards slightly tilted (-6° / +4° / -3°), drawn with hand-drawn outlines, not
    geometric perfection. Soft brick-gray #6E6259 drop shadows offset 2-3px to
    suggest lift.

- **Top-right area**: gentle whitespace with a few tiny phosphor-green dots /
  sparkles for visual rhythm. A small mist-blue #9CB4CC squiggle line as
  decorative accent, hand-drawn.
